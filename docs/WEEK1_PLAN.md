# Week 1 execution plan — 6–12 November 2026

## Purpose and boundaries

The product goal agreed on 5–6 November is a user-selected **future date 1–30 days ahead**, showing daily **mean, minimum, and maximum temperature** for one of the current five cities. The initial use is **educational and noncommercial**. Success must be reported as both mean absolute error (MAE, in °C) and the percentage of predictions within ±2 °C. These are product requirements, not capabilities or measured accuracy of the current app. The current pipeline predicts only the next day's single `Temperature_C` field, whose measurement definition and source are unknown. It cannot responsibly supply the other targets or claim skill at horizons 2–30.

Week 1's objective is to make the task, data rights, data contract, and evaluation protocol reviewable before expanding the model. This follows the problem-framing, data-checking, test-set, and error-analysis sequence in [chapter 2 of *Hands-On Machine Learning*, third edition](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/hands-on-machine-learning/9781098125967/ch02.html). Time-series validation must preserve date order, as in [scikit-learn's time-series split guidance](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.model_selection.TimeSeriesSplit.html). These sources motivate the workflow; the dates and thresholds below are our project decisions.

## Decision rules for the end of Week 1

1. **If source, rights, and target definitions are verified:** implement a source-specific ingestion contract and chronological 1–30-day evaluation in the next sprint. The app will expose only horizons whose performance has actually been measured.
2. **If source or rights remain unknown:** keep the existing one-day result labeled an internal historical benchmark, use the synthetic dataset for demos, and prioritize an authorized source. Do not manufacture min/max labels from the current `Temperature_C` column.
3. **If 30-day skill is poor:** report it by horizon; reduce the supported range or present a clearly labeled climatological estimate only after validation. Do not hide weak horizons inside a pooled average.

## Week 1 exclusions

No deep-learning upgrade, hyperparameter sweep on the 2024 holdout, arbitrary date picker advertised as working, live-weather integration, PDF export, or public accuracy promise. Each would depend on the contract and evidence above.
